The Problem

Losing a loved one is hard enough. The estate shouldn't make it harder.

In Malaysia, when someone dies without a will (intestate), families face a maze of confusion, cost, and delay. Most don't know where to begin.

⏳

Assets frozen for years

Bank accounts, property, and EPF get locked. Without the right paperwork, families wait 2–5 years — or lose the assets entirely to unclaimed estate.

💸

Lawyers cost a fortune

A simple Letter of Administration through a lawyer can cost RM 2,000–8,000+ — money grieving families often can't spare.

❓

Nobody explains the rules

Faraid? Distribution Act? Amanah Raya or High Court? Most families have no idea what they're entitled to or where to file.
